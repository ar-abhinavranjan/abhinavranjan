AR. Abhinav Ranjan

A timeline of his growth, innovation, and creation.

1

1. Idea of AR Tech (2019)

The journey began with the idea of AR Tech—exploring augmented reality, computing, emerging technologies, and futuristic technology concepts. This early vision became the foundation for a much larger technological and entrepreneurial journey.

2

2. Idea of Luminary Technicals (2021)

A larger vision began taking shape with the idea of Luminary Technicals, envisioned as a futuristic technology organization bringing together software, infrastructure, cybersecurity, research, education, and emerging technologies.

3

3. Founded Luminary Servers & Structured Luminary Technicals (2022)

The vision moved from concepts toward real systems with the establishment of Luminary Servers and the further structuring of Luminary Technicals. This phase marked the beginning of developing a more organized technology ecosystem.

4

4. Expansion of Systems & Ideas (2024)

During 2024, the ecosystem entered a major expansion phase. Technology systems, projects, research concepts, infrastructure, development ideas, and collaborative initiatives continued to grow, creating the foundations for a broader multidisciplinary organization.

5

5. Official Launch of Luminary Technicals (2025)

In 2025, Luminary Technicals moved into its public launch phase, bringing together technology development, digital infrastructure, cybersecurity, education, research, and emerging innovation under a unified organizational identity.

6

6. Expansion Across Divisions & Projects (2026)

In 2026, the organization expanded across multiple specialized areas through Luminary Servers, Luminary Developers, Luminary Cares, and Luminary Kits, while also developing a wider portfolio of technology, education, research, social-impact, and public-interest initiatives.

7

7. AR. Industries, Publishing & Major Project Expansion (2026)

The year 2026 also saw the growing role of AR. Industries alongside the development of major projects including Luminary Books, Luminary Webs, Luminary Wishes, Luminary Federals, James Web, and AgroScan AI. These initiatives expanded the ecosystem beyond conventional software development into publishing, cybersecurity education, digital experiences, research, public affairs, and socially useful technology.

8

8. Artificial Intelligence & Next-Generation Technology (2026)

The introduction of projects such as Luminary AI Engine marked a deeper move into artificial intelligence, intelligent systems, automation, machine learning, and emerging computational technologies. Together with AgroScan AI and other research initiatives, this phase established AI as an increasingly important part of the wider technological vision.

9

9. The Future — Expansion Across Every Sector

The journey continues with plans and ongoing development across cybersecurity, artificial intelligence, software, infrastructure, research, education, agriculture, public safety, digital governance, social impact, publishing, communications, data science, automation, and future technology ventures. New projects, research programs, products, and initiatives are expected to emerge as the ecosystem continues to evolve from its early AR Tech foundations into a broad multidisciplinary technology and innovation organization.
