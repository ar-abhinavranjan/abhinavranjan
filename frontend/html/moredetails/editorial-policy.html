Editorial Policy & Research Standards

Commitment to technical accuracy, primary sourcing, and transparent research publication.

1. Core Publishing Principles

All research papers, cybersecurity analyses, and technical publications hosted under the Luminary Technicals ecosystem are authored by AR. Abhinav Ranjan and collaborative technical contributors. Content is produced to advance open security research, digital safety, and educational awareness.

2. Verification & Technical Rigor

Prior to publication, all code examples, threat models, and architectural blueprints undergo empirical testing. Vulnerability disclosures follow industry-standard SecSDLC practices and responsible disclosure frameworks.

3. Corrections & Transparency

Technical accuracy is paramount. When errors or updated threat telemetry emerge, articles are updated with visible revision timestamps (dateModified). Readers may submit technical corrections directly via the Contact Page.

4. AI & Originality Disclaimer

Publications represent original research and technical syntheses. AI tools are utilized strictly for language editing, structured data validation, and computational simulation—never for factual invention.
